How to hop

Hold to charge your jump and release to hop. Match the gold aim marker for a perfect landing. Keep moving before the platform timer runs out. Tap or stroke your bunny on the home screen to pet it.

Your bunny collection

Earn gold by playing and use it to open capsules. A bunny's skin, quality, and aura roll independently. Capsules can contain duplicates. The game shows exact odds before opening capsules and from the shop. Eligible duplicate copies can be released for a little gold. Virtual items have no cash value and cannot be traded or cashed out.

Purchases

Cloudhop has no ads. Optional purchases give fixed quantities of consumable jetpacks, extra lives, gold boosts, or polish. Gentle Polish guarantees one Dirty-to-Normal upgrade; Star Polish guarantees one Normal-to-Shiny upgrade. You choose the bunny and confirm use of the item. Its skin and aura stay the same.

Apple shows the localized price and asks you to confirm checkout. We do not receive your payment card details. Purchases never automatically repeat and are not subscriptions.

Missing purchase?

Open Shop and tap Recover Purchases to recover an unfinished transaction. Confirm that you are using the Apple Account that made the purchase and that your device is online. Already fulfilled consumables cannot be restored after deleting the app. Do not delete the app while resolving a missing purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

Bunnies, gold, item counts, settings, and scores are saved on your device. This release does not have accounts or cloud saves. Reinstalling or switching devices may lose progress and fulfilled consumables. Device backups are managed by your operating system.

Refunds

For App Store refunds and billing questions, visit Apple's purchase support. Refund decisions are handled by Apple.
