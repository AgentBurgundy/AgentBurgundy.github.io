How to hop

Hold to charge your jump and release to hop. Match the gold aim marker for a perfect landing. Keep moving before the platform timer runs out. Tap or stroke your bunny on the home screen to pet it.

Your bunny collection

Find a petal on every tenth platform during unassisted runs. One petal opens one capsule; your first welcome capsule is free. Petals cannot be bought, and gold cannot buy capsules. Skin, quality and aura roll independently. Duplicates are possible; View Odds shows the probabilities before opening. Eligible spare bunnies can be released for gold. Virtual items have no cash value and cannot be traded or cashed out.

Purchases

Cloudhop has no ads. Optional permanent cosmetic sets include a specific home sky, trail and landing effect: Petal Picnic, Stargazer, Aurora Parade and Royal Reverie. Preview the exact contents in Shop > Looks and equip them in your wardrobe. Purchases do not change capsule odds or award currency, petals or bunny copies.

Gentle Polish guarantees one Dirty-to-Normal upgrade; Star Polish guarantees one Normal-to-Shiny upgrade. Each purchase adds one bottle. Choose an owned bunny in Inventory and confirm its upgrade. Its skin and aura stay the same. Polish also has earned-gold alternatives. Jetpacks, extra lives and gold boosts cost earned gold only.

Apple displays localized prices and confirms checkout. These purchases are not subscriptions. We do not receive payment-card details.

Missing purchase?

Open Shop > Help > Restore Purchases, using the Apple Account that made the purchase while online. Permanent cosmetic sets can be restored. Confirmed consumables are not replenished after deleting the app. Keep the app installed while resolving a missing purchase.

Where is my progress saved?

Your collection, gold, petals, supplies and settings are saved on your device. Settings > Export Backup creates a snapshot you can keep in Files or iCloud Drive. Restore from File imports a snapshot; backups do not merge or sync automatically. Export a backup before reinstalling or changing devices.

Game Center leaderboard

Open Home > Ranks or Settings > Leaderboard. Connect & Share Scores is optional and shares new eligible scores with Apple under your Game Center profile. Finish an unassisted run after connecting. Jetpacks, extra lives and gold boosts exclude a run from the leaderboard. Old local best scores and imported backups are never uploaded. If a score could not upload, reconnect while online to retry. Stop Sharing Scores stops future uploads; existing public scores remain with Apple. See our privacy policy.

Refunds

For billing questions or refund requests, visit Apple's purchase support. Apple handles refund decisions.
