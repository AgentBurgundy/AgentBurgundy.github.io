Privacy policy.

This policy describes the current iOS release of Cloudhop: Bunny Adventure.

Game progress

The game saves scores, gold, bunny copies, cosmetic effects, consumable items, settings, and a purchase-fulfillment ledger on your device. No game account is required. Galactic Basic LLC does not operate a game account, analytics, or cloud-save server for this release. We do not upload your collection. Optional Game Center scores are described below.

Purchases and platform services

Optional purchases use Apple's App Store through Unity In-App Purchasing. Apple processes payment and provides product prices and transaction information to the app. The app stores product and transaction identifiers locally to deliver items and avoid duplicate grants. We do not receive your payment-card details.

This release uses the platform-native store. It does not initialize Unity Authentication, Unity Analytics, webshops, or direct-to-consumer payment services. See Apple's App Store privacy information and Unity's player privacy policy for their platform practices. Device-level crash sharing and backups are controlled by your Apple settings.

Optional Game Center leaderboard

Cloudhop uses Apple Game Center for the Best Run leaderboard. Connecting is optional. Before connecting, the game explains that new eligible scores will be sent to Apple and that your Game Center name and score can be public. Cloudhop sends only completed, unassisted run scores, after you opt in. Your collection, purchases, bunny names, and backup files are not sent to the leaderboard.

The app uses an identifier scoped to this game to keep pending scores separate between Apple players on the device. This identifier and pending scores stay in local device storage; they are not included in exported collection backups or sent to a Galactic Basic LLC server. Apple hosts the leaderboard and handles Game Center authentication.

Use Home > Ranks > Stop Sharing Scores to stop future submissions and clear locally queued scores. This does not erase scores already submitted to Apple. Manage your Game Center profile and sharing preferences in your Apple settings. See Apple's Game Center privacy information for Apple's data practices and choices.

Advertising and tracking

Cloudhop contains no advertising SDK, advertising banners, or rewarded ads. We do not use cross-app tracking or request access to an advertising identifier. No location, contacts, camera, microphone, or photo-library access is required to play.

Support

If you contact us by email, we receive your email address and information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request and troubleshoot the issue. We keep support correspondence only as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Avoid sending sensitive personal or payment information.

This website

This support website has no analytics scripts, advertising, or account forms. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical information, including IP addresses, to serve and secure the site. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Your choices

You can delete local game data by deleting the app, subject to device backups. This can also remove your progress and fulfilled consumable items. It does not cancel or refund App Store purchases. Apple handles billing and refund requests. Email us to request access to or deletion of support correspondence that we hold.

Audience

Cloudhop is a general-audience game and is not offered in Apple's Kids category. We do not knowingly ask children to provide personal information. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can address it.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when the game's data practices change. Contact Galactic Basic LLC at ronald@aibaker.io with privacy questions or requests.
